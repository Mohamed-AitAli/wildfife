LITHIUM vs AGM
BATTERIES

Is lithium worth 3× the price? We ran the 5-year math. The answer might surprise you.

📖 12 min read · Updated Feb 2026

The Real Question

The question isn't which battery is "better" — it's which battery is better for your use case and timeline. A weekend warrior who builds once and sells their van in 2 years has a very different calculation than a full-timer who will spend 5 years in their rig.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Feature LiFePO4 Lithium AGM Deep Cycle
Usable Capacity 95% (190Ah of 200Ah) 50% (100Ah of 200Ah)
Cycle Life 3,000–5,000 cycles 300–500 cycles
Weight (200Ah) ~26 lbs ~130 lbs
Upfront Cost (200Ah) $500–$800 $150–$250
5-Year Total Cost $600–$900 $700–$1,500 (replacements)
Charge Speed Very fast (accepts full charge rate) Slow (tapers significantly)
Temperature Cannot charge below 32°F Works in cold (charging)
Maintenance Zero Check water (wet) / minimal (AGM)
BMS Included Yes (quality brands) No

The 5-Year Math

Assume you're running 200Ah of storage, full-time, cycling daily:

Year Lithium Cost AGM Cost
Year 1 $650 (purchase) $200 (purchase)
Year 2 $0 $0
Year 3 $0 $200 (replace — AGM dead by now)
Year 4 $0 $0
Year 5 $0 $200 (replace again)
5-Year Total $650 $600
💡 The Verdict Over 5 years, costs are nearly identical — but lithium gives you TWICE the usable capacity and weighs 80% less. For full-timers, lithium wins clearly. For weekend warriors who'll resell quickly, AGM is totally fine.

Lithium Cold Weather Warning

LiFePO4 batteries cannot be charged below 32°F (0°C) without damaging the cells. This is a hard limit. Solutions: buy a battery with an internal heater (Battle Born and Renogy offer this), insulate the battery compartment, or use a battery with a built-in low-temperature cutoff BMS. In the desert Southwest? Never an issue. Alaska in January? Plan accordingly.

Recommended Brands

Lithium (LiFePO4)

  • Battle Born — the most popular, rock solid BMS, made in the USA, 10-year warranty
  • Ampere Time — excellent value, solid cells, lower price point
  • Renogy — good mid-range option, wide availability
  • Epoch Batteries — newer but impressive quality and value

AGM (if that's your choice)

  • Battle Born AGM — premium quality
  • VMAX Tanks — popular, good cycle life for AGM
  • Renogy Deep Cycle AGM — widely available and reliable
